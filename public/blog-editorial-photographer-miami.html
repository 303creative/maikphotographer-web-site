Editorial Photographer in Miami: What to Expect

Maikel Marshall · Cinematic Photographer · Miami, FL

Searching for an editorial photographer in Miami can be confusing — everyone calls themselves one. Here's what editorial photography actually is, and how to pick the right person for your shoot.

What "editorial" really means

Editorial photography tells a story. It's not a stiff headshot or a generic product shot — it's images with mood, direction and intention, the kind you'd see in a magazine or a premium campaign. Lighting, wardrobe, location and posing all work together.

What to look for

A point of view. A real editorial photographer directs — they don't just press the shutter. Look at whether their portfolio has a consistent, recognizable style.

Cinematic lighting. Editorial work lives and dies on light. Flat, even light looks like a passport photo; shaped, directional light looks like a story.

Full editing. The edit is half the image. Ask what's included and how fast you get the final gallery.

What a session looks like

A good session starts before the camera: we align on the concept, references, wardrobe and location. On the day, I direct every frame. After, you get a fully edited gallery in about 48 hours — ready to publish.

Who it's for

Models building a portfolio, creators leveling up their feed, and brands that want to look premium. If you want images that sell — not just pictures — that's editorial.
