Courthouse Wedding Photography in Miami

Maikel Marshall · Cinematic Photographer · Miami, FL

A courthouse wedding is small, fast and personal — and the photos are exactly what you'll keep forever. Here's how to make sure your day is captured beautifully in Miami.

Why courthouse weddings are worth photographing

They're intimate and real. There's no huge production, so the images are honest — the look, the nerves, the first kiss as newlyweds. Small doesn't mean less important; it means every frame counts.

How much coverage do you need?

Most courthouse weddings need about 1 hour: the ceremony moments plus portraits right after. If you want more looks, we can add a second location — one of my favorites is the bridge to Key Biscayne with the Miami skyline behind you.

What you get

Editorial coverage of the ceremony and portraits, a full gallery of edited high-resolution photos, and — if you want it — a short cinematic drone video of your day.

How to book

Tell me your date and roughly how many hours you need. I'll confirm availability and send the details. Courthouse coverage is straightforward and stress-free — you focus on the moment, I handle the images.
