[04] FAQ •
How long does a website take?

A focused service site is typically a few weeks depending on scope, pages and how fast content and approvals move. We confirm the timeline in the scope before we start.

Do I own the website and accounts?

Yes. We hand over the site and every account (hosting, domain, analytics, booking) at launch — you own all of it.

Can you add online booking?

Yes. We wire a booking tool or a contextual inquiry form to match how you actually take clients, and connect it to your calendar or CRM when needed.
